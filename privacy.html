Steel Fang Privacy Policy

Developer: Anay Tripathi · Last updated: 20 October 2026

Summary

The game itself does not collect or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. If you buy gems or coins, the only details the developer receives are the ones you send with your order (see Purchases).

Data saved on your device

The game saves your settings (mode, tank class, graphics, sound), your best score, your coins, gems and cannons, and your random player ID in your device's local storage so they are remembered next time. This data never leaves your device, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Purchases

Players can buy gems and coins by paying with a UPI app such as Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay or BHIM. The payment happens entirely inside that UPI app; Steel Fang never sees bank, card or UPI details. To receive a purchase, the player sends their order code and a payment screenshot to the developer, who replies with a gift code. That message, including the sender's phone number and the screenshot, is used only to deliver the purchase and is not shared with anyone.

The game creates a random player ID on the device so that a gift code works only for the player who paid. The player ID contains no personal information.

Network requests

The game's lettering fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When that happens, Google receives the standard technical details any website request sends, such as your IP address. Steel Fang itself receives nothing. Google's own privacy policy applies to that request.

Children

Steel Fang collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Device permissions

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files. Gamepads are read only while you play, to control your tank.
